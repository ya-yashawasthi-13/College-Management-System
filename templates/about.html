About Graphic Era University

Over 3 Decades of Academic Excellence, Innovation and Transformative Leadership

Our Heritage & Legacy

In 1993, visionary academician and computer scientist Prof. (Dr.) Kamal Ghanshala embarked on a mission to transform the higher education landscape in the hill state of Uttarakhand by founding the Graphic Era Institute of Technology (GEIT).

Through unwavering dedication to educational rigor, world-class infrastructure, and state-of-the-art laboratory development, GEIT rapidly earned national prominence. In 2008, the Ministry of Human Resource Development (now Ministry of Education), Government of India, conferred upon it the status of Deemed to be University under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956.

Today, Graphic Era (Deemed to be University) stands as one of India's preeminent multi-disciplinary universities, ranked among the Top 55 universities nationally in NIRF rankings, and accredited with the prestigious Grade 'A+' by NAAC.
